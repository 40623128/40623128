## Current Research

### Natural Convection over a Heated Circular Plate

I investigate the development of natural-convection structures above finite heated circular plates, including:

* Laminar boundary-layer development near the plate edge
* Transitional thermal structures
* Formation and interaction of turbulent plumes
* Rayleigh-number-dependent flow transitions
* Local and area-averaged Nusselt-number distributions
* Spectral and statistical analysis of unsteady thermal structures

The numerical simulations are performed using high-order finite-volume methods and direct numerical simulation.

### Heated Rotating Disk

My current work also examines the coupling between:

* Buoyancy-driven natural convection
* Rotation-induced radial and tangential flow
* Temperature-dependent viscosity
* Heat-transfer enhancement or suppression
* Transition between natural-, forced-, and mixed-convection regimes
